Title: Sequence diagram — User Registration/Login flow
Type: mermaid

sequenceDiagram
    actor User
    participant AuthController
    participant AuthRepositoryImpl
    participant HiveService
    User->>AuthController: enter credentials
    AuthController->>AuthRepositoryImpl: login(phone, pin)
    AuthRepositoryImpl->>HiveService: fetch Agent
    HiveService-->>AuthRepositoryImpl: AgentEntity
    AuthRepositoryImpl-->>AuthController: Success
    AuthController-->>User: Navigate to /sales
