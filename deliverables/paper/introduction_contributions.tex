\section{Introduction}
Insider threat detection using machine learning heavily relies on realistic evaluation protocols. Legacy approaches on the LANL dataset achieved near-perfect PR-AUC by sampling benign events primarily from the beginning of the timeline while malicious events occurred later, introducing severe temporal leakage.

\subsection{Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item A documented case study of how time-correlated sampling inflates detection metrics.
    \item A leakage-aware chronological protocol enforcing a purge gap and block-bootstrap CIs.
    \item A reusable audit checklist based on leakage probes.
\end{enumerate}
