\section{Limitations}
This study is subject to several limitations: extreme class imbalance; a small positive count of red-team events; data originates from a single network (2015-era); relies solely on the authentication modality. Furthermore, the red-team exercise is not synonymous with insider behaviour. Failed logons are included only for users with at least one successful logon, and the real timeframe is undisclosed. We also acknowledge caveats regarding sampling/re-weighting and unmatched red-team events.
