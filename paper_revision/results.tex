\section{Results}
Due to environmental constraints, full measured distributions will be reported after the complete 12GB pipeline run is executed on a local workstation. The methodology proposed herein outlines the expected evaluation criteria.

Once executed, we expect the time-only baseline to achieve high PR-AUC under the naive random split (P1) and user-grouped split (P2) due to temporal leakage, and subsequently collapse to the true prevalence baseline under the strict chronological split (P3) and rolling-origin split (P4).
