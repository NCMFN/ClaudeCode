# Micro-Pricing Dynamics & Revenue Infrastructure

Implement a full supervised learning research pipeline to model micro-pricing dynamics across UK National Rail stations. Evaluates whether Decision Tree Regressors can replace manual corporate pricing policy by predicting optimal ticket prices at a transaction level, and identifies pricing anomalies where human intervention remains essential.

## Setup
1. `pip install -r requirements.txt`
2. `python src/main.py`
